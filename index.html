Background & Expertise

I am a Senior Backend Engineer & Technical Lead with over 12 years of experience designing, architecting, and scaling high-traffic distributed systems supporting millions of active users. Most recently, I led core backend engineering initiatives at Matrimony.com Limited, driving the migration of mission-critical monolithic modules into Node.js and GraphQL microservices, architecting high-volume authentication flows, and achieving a 30% latency reduction across core APIs via deep query tuning and Redis caching.

Combining a battle-tested systems architecture foundation with Generative AI & Agentic Engineering, I build governed, resilient AI systems using FastAPI, LangChain, LangGraph, CrewAI, and the Model Context Protocol (MCP). My focus centers on multi-agent collaboration, deterministic guardrails, structured evaluation harnesses, and robust production-ready workflows.
